# Loader Operations Workflow

**Simon Peter Mukooza | Dubai International Airport, Terminals 1 and 3**

I developed this workflow during my time supervising ramp operations at Dubai International Airport, working in coordination with a fellow supervisor. It brings loader deployment, resource planning, disruption response and shift handovers into one structured process.

The method follows four stages: **Create, Select, Plan, Manage**. The document includes the full workflow table, showing how those stages connect during a shift. It also shows how I account for workload fairness, breaks, prayer times and clear communication in a multilingual team.

This work demonstrates my approach to operational systems: understand the conditions, make priorities explicit, give people a practical plan, and preserve continuity through monitoring and handover.
